## 📋 Summary of Current Positioning

### What We NOW Clearly Communicate:

✅ **Teach Nations is an independent organization**
   - Not a branch, chapter, or representative of TEN
   - Based in Uganda, serving East Africa
   - Operating under our own organizational structure

✅ **Partnership, Not Ownership**
   - We partner with Teach Every Nation to deliver their courses
   - TEN courses (like Bible School on Wheels) are part of our broader program offering
   - We acknowledge TEN as the course creator and maintain quality standards

✅ **Broader Mission**
   - Schools Discipleship Programs (with Scripture Union)
   - Community Development initiatives
   - Mission Trips to Uganda
   - TEN course delivery (one program among many)
