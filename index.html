About Teach Nations

Teach Nations is a Christian discipleship and leadership movement committed to developing individuals who live out biblical truth and lead with integrity, purpose, and excellence through biblical education, leadership development, professional training, community outreach, and mission engagement.

Our Bible School on Wheels program is delivered in partnership with Teach Every Nation, alongside our own Flourish, Marksman, Transformers, and Leadership & Professional Development programs across Uganda and East Africa.

MissionTo make disciples, equip leaders, and transform communities through biblical education, leadership development, and intentional discipleship.

VisionTo see generations of Christ-centered leaders transforming nations.

Our Commitment

Teach Nations exists to build a movement of biblically grounded, purpose-driven, and socially responsible leaders across Uganda and East Africa. Through our partnership with Teach Every Nation for course delivery, alongside our own discipleship programs and community outreaches, we demonstrate the love of Christ in word and deed—transforming individuals, schools, and communities.
